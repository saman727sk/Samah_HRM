Build a modern, fast, responsive marketing website for **Samah HRM**, a cloud HR and payroll software for small and medium businesses in Pakistan. The website's goal is to get companies to **start a free 14-day trial** (no card needed).

## Product facts (use only these, do not invent features)

Samah HRM is a web app that runs in any browser, on computer or phone. Each company gets its own private workspace. There are four kinds of users: employees, managers or team leads, HR admins, and help desk or payroll staff.

**Attendance**
- Check-in and check-out from the browser, verified by office location (geofence radius).
- Work-from-home check-in only on approved WFH days.
- Late, half-day, absent and overtime are worked out automatically from each person's shift.
- Forgotten check-outs are closed automatically and the person is notified.
- Employees can request attendance corrections, and the original punches are always kept.
- Managers and HR can record attendance for someone who couldn't check in.

**Leave and work from home**
- Leave types with balances: annual, casual, sick, unpaid, maternity, paternity and bereavement, all editable.
- Full days, half days or hours.
- A live policy check while applying: notice period, balance, probation, overlaps, and a warning when too many teammates are off.
- WFH requests by date or weekly pattern, with weekly and monthly limits.
- Overtime claims.

**Approvals**
- Multi-step approval flows: manager, then the manager's manager, then HR.
- Requests that wait too long escalate automatically.
- Notifications in the app and by email.

**Rota and shifts**
- Managers plan their team's weekly shifts and days off on a grid, and can copy last week in one click.
- The rota drives attendance: lateness is judged against the planned shift.
- Employees see "My shifts: next 7 days".

**Tasks**
- Managers assign tasks with a due date and priority to one person or a whole team.
- Each employee gets a morning "Your tasks for today" reminder.
- Each task has its own chat between the employee and the manager, and statuses: to do, in progress, done, blocked.
- A daily work log with hours, links and files.

**Payroll** (built for Pakistan, in PKR)
- Monthly payroll from attendance, leave, overtime, salary structures and loans.
- Income tax slabs.
- A review step that lists anything to check, approval, then locking.
- Bank transfer file (CSV with IBANs).
- Payslips as PDFs with the company logo.
- Salary revisions with effective dates.
- Loans and salary advances with monthly installments.

**HR tools**
- Employee records with custom fields.
- Import hundreds of employees from Excel or CSV, and export them back.
- Teams and managers.
- Per-person access rights: e.g. a payroll manager or a support manager without giving full HR access.
- Switch features on or off per employee.
- HR dashboard with today's attendance board, department charts and alerts.
- CSV attendance reports.

**Help desk**
- Employees "Ask HR" about salary, leave, IT, documents and more, and HR replies in a thread.

**Policies as data**
- Every rule (leave, WFH, shifts, payroll, approvals) can be changed by HR with an effective date, a preview of who is affected, and full history with rollback.

**Security and trust**
- Each company's data is separate.
- Optional two-step sign-in with an emailed code.
- Every change is recorded in an audit log that can't be edited.
- Locked payroll months can't be changed.
- Passwords are stored hashed.

**Other**
- English and Urdu interface.
- Light and dark mode.
- Notifications in the app and by email.

**Do not mention:** a mobile app, selfie check-in or automatic check-in. They are not available yet.

## FAQ content

- **Is there a free trial?** Yes, 14 days with every feature, no card needed.
- **Do employees need to install anything?** No. It works in the browser on any phone or computer.
- **Can I import my employees from Excel?** Yes. Download the template, fill it in and upload it. You see a preview before anything is saved.
- **Does payroll follow Pakistani tax?** It includes income tax slabs and PKR. HR can edit the rules, and they should be checked with your accountant.
- **Can a manager see only their own team?** Yes. Managers see only the people under them, and HR decides each manager's rights.
- **Is my data safe?** Each company's data is separate, every change is logged, and sign-in can require an emailed code.
- **Can I change plans later?** Yes, upgrade any time.

## Design

- **Font:** "Inter" or "Plus Jakarta Sans" from Google Fonts.
- **Feel:** professional, clean and friendly, like a modern SaaS site such as Linear, Notion or BambooHR. Plenty of white space and clear hierarchy.
- **Layout:** mobile-first. It must look great on phones (most visitors in Pakistan are on mobile), with a sticky header and a **Start free trial** button.
- **Images:** product UI mock-ups built from simple HTML or CSS, or clearly marked placeholders. No stock photos of fake teams.
- **Accessibility:** good contrast and keyboard-friendly.
- **Speed:** fast-loading with no heavy libraries. Add simple fade-in on scroll.
